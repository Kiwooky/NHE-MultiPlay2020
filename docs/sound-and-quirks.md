# Sound and quirks

**Lo-fi but lush: grainy, slightly breathy repeats that bend in pitch whenever anything moves.**

## Sound

- **Grain that follows the note.** 8-bit converters inside a 2:1 NE570 compander: quiet between notes, fizz on the echoes, grittier tails.
- **Breathing.** The compander's 4.7 ms envelope pumps a little on attacks and decays.
- **Pitch-bending sweeps.** Delay Time changes the sample clock, so knob moves and LFO sweeps warble like tape.
- **Darker, dirtier repeats.** Every pass goes back through the filters, the compander and the converters.
- **Long delays get grimier.** At 2 s the clock is about 33 kHz while the filters stay near 19 kHz, so a little aliasing leaks through.
- **Flange and chorus with an edge.** A sharp, jet-like flanger on 16 ms; a wobbly chorus/double on 250 ms.
- **Wide stereo.** Output 2's echoes are inverted: wide in stereo, cancelled in mono.

## Quirks of the original controls

| Control | Quirk |
| --- | --- |
| Delay Time | Taper bunches up at the long end; any move bends pitch |
| Width | Not a depth knob: a crossfade from Delay Time to the LFO. At full Width, Delay Time does nothing |
| Speed | A 1 Hz filter after the LFO rounds the wave and shrinks depth as Speed rises |
| Regen | Factory-trimmed just under self-oscillation |
| Mix | On the original it only worked on Output 2; Output 1 was a fixed 1:1 mix |
| Repeat Hold | The clock keeps running, so a frozen loop still varispeeds |
| Bypass | The delay keeps running underneath; a held loop is still there when you switch back on |
| Internal trims | Regen ceiling, compander bias, clock floor (owners stretch the long range to 4 s or more) |

## Who used it

A cult pedal, mostly in noise-rock and experimental rigs: Jonathan Hischke (Hella, Dot Hacker) called it his favourite pedal "because it makes me think differently"; Juan Alderete (Racer X, The Mars Volta); Nathan Latona and Nick Reinhart (Tera Melos); Masahiko Ohno (Solmania).
